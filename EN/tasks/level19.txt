(files, TLS, PTY, UNIX sockets). There is NO nc here — only socat.

fileserver serves a file on TCP port 4450.

GOAL: grab the password for level20 using socat.

Hint: socat connects two addresses. One end is stdout (-), the other
      is TCP:<host>:<port>. Route the server's stream to your screen
