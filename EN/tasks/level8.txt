
============================================================
 LEVEL 8  —  base64 over netcat   (PART 2: encoding)
============================================================
A binary breaks over a "raw" channel: NUL bytes, \r\n, telnet modes.
base64 fixes it — the channel only ever sees text.

On fileserver, port 4447 hands out data ENCODED in base64.

GOAL: receive and decode (password for level9).

Hint: base64 can also go the other way (flag -d). Pipe receive into
      decode.
