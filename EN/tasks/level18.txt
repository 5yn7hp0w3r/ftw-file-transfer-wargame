 LEVEL 18  —  UDP netcat
============================================================
TCP blocked by a firewall? UDP sometimes gets through. It is
connectionless: the server stays silent until it receives at least one
datagram from you.

fileserver listens on UDP port 4449 and, once it "sees" you, sends
the file.

GOAL: get the password for level19 over UDP.

Hint: nc speaks UDP via -u. The server must first "see" your datagram
      — send something (even a blank line) so it learns your address,
      then receive the reply. A timeout (-w) keeps you from hanging.
