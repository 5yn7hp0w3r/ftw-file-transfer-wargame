── tar over a pipe ───────────────────────────────────────
tar streams to stdout, netcat streams over the wire — pipe
them together and a whole directory tree moves in one shot,
preserving permissions, symlinks and timestamps.

  # sender
  tar czf - dir | nc -lvnp 4444
  # receiver
  nc host 4444 | tar xzf -

  c create · x extract · z gzip · f - use stdout/stdin

No temp archive touches disk. Same pattern with ssh instead
of nc is the classic `tar czf - dir | ssh host 'tar xzf -'`.
