── port discovery (GHOST) ────────────────────────────────
Services don't have to sit on well-known ports. Finding a
listener on a high, "random" port is basic recon — and you can
do it without nmap:

  nc -zv host 9000-9100          -z scan (no data) -v report
  for p in $(seq 9000 9100); do (echo >/dev/tcp/host/$p) \
      2>/dev/null && echo open $p; done          # pure bash

Real engagements: nmap / masscan / rustscan sweep ports fast.
The lesson is the habit — when something "should be there but
isn't on 80", go looking for where it actually lives.
