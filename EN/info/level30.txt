── recognising encodings (GHOST) ─────────────────────────
Half of decoding is identifying WHAT you're looking at. Tells:

  charset A-Za-z0-9+/ with '=' padding   → base64
  A-Z2-7 with '='                         → base32
  only 0-9a-f, even length                → hex
  '-'/'_' instead of '+'/'/'              → base64url
  high entropy, no structure              → compressed/encrypted

Tools to interrogate a blob:
  file -            guess type from magic bytes
  xxd | head        eyeball the first bytes / header
  ent / entropy     randomness (packed vs plaintext)

Guess, decode, sanity-check the output, iterate.
