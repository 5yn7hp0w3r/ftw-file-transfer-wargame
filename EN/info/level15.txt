── uuencode / uudecode ───────────────────────────────────
A pre-base64 way to push binaries through 7-bit text channels
(email, Usenet, terminals). Encodes 3 bytes into 4 printable
chars, wrapped in a header/footer:

  begin 644 secret.txt      <- mode + original filename
  <encoded lines>
  end

  uuencode file name > out.uu     encode
  uudecode out.uu                 recreates 'name'

From the sharutils package. Largely historical now, but you
still meet it in legacy mail flows and old exam boxes.
