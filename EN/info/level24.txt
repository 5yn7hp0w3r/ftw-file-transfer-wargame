── zlib (raw DEFLATE) ────────────────────────────────────
Same DEFLATE algorithm as gzip, but with zlib's tiny 2-byte
header + Adler-32 checksum instead of the gzip wrapper. That
difference matters: `gunzip` refuses raw zlib streams.

Where it hides: PNG chunks, PDF stream objects, the ZIP format,
HTTP "deflate" encoding, many binary protocols and game saves.

  python3 -c 'import zlib,sys;sys.stdout.buffer.write(zlib.decompress(sys.stdin.buffer.read()))'
  openssl zlib -d        (if built with zlib support)

Recognising "this blob is zlib, not gzip" is half the battle.
