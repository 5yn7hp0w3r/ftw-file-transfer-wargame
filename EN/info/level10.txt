── hex encoding ──────────────────────────────────────────
The simplest byte-to-text scheme: each byte becomes two hex
digits (0–9 a–f). 100% size overhead, but trivially readable
and copy-pasteable by hand — great for small blobs and for
eyeballing binary data.

  xxd file           annotated hex + ASCII dump
  xxd -p             plain hex, no offsets/ASCII
  xxd -r -p          reverse plain hex back to bytes
  od -An -tx1        alternative dump
  echo 4142 | xxd -r -p   ->  AB

Same idea as \xHH escapes in shells and languages.
