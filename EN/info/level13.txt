── ncat --ssl (encrypted channel) ───────────────────────
Sometimes you encrypt the CHANNEL, not the file. ncat (from
nmap) can wrap a plain TCP transfer in TLS:

  ncat --ssl -lvnp 4444 < secret     # server, self-signed cert
  ncat --ssl host 4444 > out         # client

The cert is self-signed, so clients warn — fine for ad-hoc
transfers, and it defeats passive sniffing on the wire. This
is the difference between hiding the *contents in transit*
(TLS) vs hiding the *file at rest* (openssl enc, level 12).
socat can do the same with OPENSSL address types.
