── gawk networking (/inet) ───────────────────────────────
GNU awk has a little-known superpower: it can open TCP/UDP
sockets through special filenames.

  "/inet/tcp/0/host/port"      (0 = any local port)
  cmd |& s                     two-way coprocess to socket s

You literally hand-write the HTTP request and read the raw
reply — which arrives WITH status line and headers, so you
strip everything up to the first blank line to get the body.

Obscure, but on a hardened box with almost nothing installed,
"but it has awk" can be the whole exfil path.
