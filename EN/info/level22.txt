── bzip2 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
bzip2 compresses in blocks using the Burrows–Wheeler transform
plus move-to-front and Huffman coding. It usually beats gzip on
ratio, at the cost of being slower and more memory-hungry.

  bzip2 file        -> file.bz2
  bunzip2 file.bz2  or:  bzcat file.bz2

Pipes like the rest:  curl -s url | bunzip2
Between gzip (fast) and xz (smallest), bzip2 is the middle
ground you still see on older packages and mirrors.
