── socat ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
socat = "netcat on steroids". It connects two *addresses* and
pumps bytes between them — and it understands far more address
types than nc:

  TCP / TCP-LISTEN   UDP        OPEN:file        EXEC:'cmd'
  PTY (real ttys)    UNIX-*     OPENSSL / OPENSSL-LISTEN

  socat - TCP:host:4450                 fetch to stdout
  socat TCP-LISTEN:4450,reuseaddr,fork OPEN:file,rdonly

fork handles multiple clients; reuseaddr avoids "address in
use". socat gives fully-interactive TTY reverse shells and
encrypted relays that nc can't do alone.
