── xz / LZMA ─────────────────────────────────────────────
xz wraps LZMA2 — currently the best general-purpose ratio of
the common tools, which is why the Linux kernel and many
distros ship .xz packages. Trade-off: slow, RAM-heavy compress
(decompress is cheap).

  xz file          -> file.xz
  unxz file.xz     or:  xz -d file.xz   ·   xzcat file.xz
  xz -9e           extreme compression

Related formats share the family: lzma, lzip. When size on the
wire matters more than CPU, xz wins.
