── base32 ────────────────────────────────────────────────
Base32 (RFC 4648) uses 32 symbols: A–Z and 2–7. It packs 5
bytes into 8 characters — less dense than base64 (~60% over-
head) but with real advantages:

  * case-insensitive — survives systems that upper/lower text
  * no + / that break URLs, filenames, DNS labels
  * hand-transcribable, works in QR codes and voice

You'll see it in TOTP/2FA secrets, onion addresses, and DNS-
based exfiltration. Decode with `base32 -d` (or basenc).
