── UDP with netcat ───────────────────────────────────────
UDP is connectionless: no handshake, no delivery guarantees,
no ordering. A UDP "listener" doesn't know a client exists
until it receives the first datagram — which learns the peer's
address so it can reply.

  nc -u -lvnp 4444        UDP listen
  nc -u host 4444         UDP client (send something first!)

Why bother: firewalls that block inbound TCP sometimes pass
UDP (DNS/53, NTP/123). Downsides: packets can drop or reorder,
so it's unreliable for big files without your own retry logic.
