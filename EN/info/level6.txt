── netcat (nc) ───────────────────────────────────────────
The "TCP/IP swiss army knife". One side listens, the other
connects; netcat doesn't care who is attacker or victim, only
who initiates.

  nc -lvnp 4444        listen: -l listen -v verbose
                       -n no DNS -p port
  nc host 4444         connect (client)
  -N / -q 0            close after EOF (version-dependent)

Flavours differ: OpenBSD nc, GNU traditional, busybox nc, and
nmap's ncat — flags and features vary, so check `nc -h`.
The bedrock of ad-hoc transfers, bind/reverse shells, scans.
