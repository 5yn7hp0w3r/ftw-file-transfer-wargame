── base85 / Ascii85 ──────────────────────────────────────
Base85 encodes 4 bytes into 5 printable chars, so overhead is
only ~25% — noticeably tighter than base64's ~33%. It uses an
85-character alphabet (enough that 85^5 > 256^4).

Variants: Adobe Ascii85 (PDF), btoa, RFC 1924, ZeroMQ Z85.
You'll meet it in PDF streams, PostScript, git binary diffs.

  python3 -c 'import base64,sys;sys.stdout.buffer.write(base64.b85encode(sys.stdin.buffer.read()))'
  ...b85decode(...)  to reverse

Denser text encoding when every byte on the wire counts.
