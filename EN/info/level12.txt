── openssl enc (symmetric encryption) ───────────────────
Encoding ≠ encryption. base64 anyone reverses; AES needs the
key. openssl's `enc` does symmetric crypto from the CLI.

  openssl enc -aes-256-cbc -pbkdf2 -salt -base64 -k KEY   # encrypt
  openssl enc -d ...same flags...                          # decrypt

  -aes-256-cbc  cipher+mode      -pbkdf2  derive key from
  -salt         random salt      passphrase (many iterations)
  -base64       armour output    -k KEY   passphrase

Gotcha: encrypt and decrypt MUST use identical parameters
(cipher, kdf, salt handling). CBC gives confidentiality, not
integrity — real protocols add a MAC / use AEAD (GCM).
